One connected story

From documents to understanding.

Start with a realistic sample business or supported files. The first useful result should take only a few deliberate steps.

01

Explore

Read believable sample invoices, bills and receipts before using them.

02

Bring documents

Choose the bundle or your files. Confirm your business if needed.

03

Recognize

AI identifies the document, parties and meaning. No type labels to fill in.

04

Understand

See income, costs, profit or loss, recorded cash and known open amounts.

05

Ask & review

Check a cited answer. Confirm a match or resolve a repeated bill.

06

Export

Take away the overview, records and unresolved items. Clear and start again.

What the visitor gets

A workspace they can check.

AI recognizes and explains. Code calculates the figures. The visitor stays in control of record changes.

The moment that makes it click

“Why did we invoice $4,800
but receive only $3,000?”

The copilot opens the invoice and payment evidence. The visitor reviews the proposed match and sees which amount remains explicitly open.

A payment match resolves the relationship.

Income stays $4,800. Recorded cash stays $3,000. The same sale is counted once. A repeated supplier bill also keeps two sources and one cost.

A familiar backbone

Built like the other two demos.

The structure and operating standards come from the actual ClipBuilder and ScreenPilot repositories.

The same engineering foundation

A pnpm workspace with a Next.js/React frontend, an Express API and shared validated contracts. One shared money engine keeps calculations and review consistent.

OpenAI is the proposed first live adapter. Gemini remains a deliberate alternative. Prepared samples work without paid inference.

A focused first demo

  • One business, a small bounded document collection and a temporary browser session.
  • Separate currencies; clear coverage; no hidden AI calls when browsing, reviewing or exporting.
  • Provider credentials stay on the private backend. No persistent document library is required.
  • The copilot explains records and calculations; it provides no financial, tax or investment advice.
Scope and next decisions

A complete demo, deliberately bounded.

First build

Landing, sample previews, recognition, overview, records, review, insights, copilot, manual entry, export and reset.

Later possibilities

A simple invoice builder, more evaluated document profiles and a read-only MCP adapter. Bank sync, payments and a persistent SaaS need separate scope.

Before live release

Confirm models and budget, tested formats, cloud region and final design. The intended domain is retrosum.com; ownership is unconfirmed.
