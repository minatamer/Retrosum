Your session assistant

Meet Retro.
More than a chat box.

Retro summarizes your records, explains the dashboard and gives a short guided tour. It can also prepare invoices, expenses, payment records and contacts from a plain-language request.

Watch Retro scroll to a chart, outline a field, and fill an editable form. Confirm once, and the records and charts update together. A small Undo restores the latest action. Refresh preserves this workspace; Reset or the fixed two-hour expiry ends it.

New invoices begin as drafts. Issuing and recording payment are separate. Originals are retained only in temporary browser storage; live AI processing sends the required content to OpenAI, without a server upload library.
